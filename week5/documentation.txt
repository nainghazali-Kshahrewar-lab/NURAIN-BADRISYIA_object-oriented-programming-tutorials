1. Why are the variables declared as private?

→ The variables are declared as private to protect the student's information.
They cannot be accessed or changed directly from outside the Student class.
Instead, getters and setters are used to control access to the data.


2. What happens if the variables are declared public?

→ If the variables are public, other classes can directly access and change
the student's information. This reduces data protection and does not properly
demonstrate encapsulation.


3. Why do we use getters and setters?

→ Getters are used to retrieve the values of private variables, while setters
are used to modify their values. They provide controlled access to the data.
